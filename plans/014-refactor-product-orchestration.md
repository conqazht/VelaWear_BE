## Why

`ProductServiceImpl` owns CRUD/query orchestration and also assembles deeply contextual responses using translations, categories, images, variants, and effective pricing. An explicit assembler can isolate deterministic presentation policy, but this mapping is not simple field copying and must not hide I/O or become per-product N+1 work.

- `src/main/java/vn/conganh/commercial/feature/storefrontcatalog/StorefrontCatalogServiceImpl.java` owns separate storefront visibility/catalog behavior that must remain separate.
