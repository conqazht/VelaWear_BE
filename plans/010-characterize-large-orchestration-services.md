## Why

Auth, checkout, sale-campaign, and product services combine orchestration, persistence, policy, mapping, localization, and token/pricing logic. Extracting collaborators without first freezing observable behavior risks subtle regressions in security, concurrency, money, and locale semantics. Focused characterization creates the safety net for four later refactor PRs.
